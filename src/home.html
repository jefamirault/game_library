How sharing works

A library is a spreadsheet plus a Drive folder of ROMs, in your own Drive. Your Google sharing decides who can see it: share the spreadsheet and the ROMs folder, or open the library and use Share & manage, which does both.

Viewers can browse and download. Editors can also add and delete games, upload covers, and share.
